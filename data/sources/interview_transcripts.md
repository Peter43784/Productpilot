# Interview transcripts — Customer calls (January 2026)

## Interview 1 — Head of Ops, Acme Retail (Jan 12)
Interviewer: What happened during your first month?
Customer: The trial setup took too long — the wizard wanted 9 fields and our ops person
gave up twice. Once we got in, the reports were fine. But honestly the first run
experience nearly lost us.

## Interview 2 — PM at Northwind (Jan 13)
Interviewer: Why did your team consider cancelling in month 2?
Customer: Month 1 was great — activation email, guided tour, clear value. Month 2
nothing. No push notification when a report threshold broke, no digest that was useful.
We wanted alerts in Slack. The value just disappeared.

## Interview 3 — Data lead at Globex (Jan 15)
Interviewer: What is blocking company-wide rollout?
Customer: SSO. Security wants SSO before we roll out to 400 people. Also our finance
team relies on the legacy CSV export and it has been broken since your API v2 migration.
Deprecate it properly with a migration path and we will be happier.

## Interview 4 — Founder at TinyCo (Jan 18)
Interviewer: Any feedback on onboarding?
Customer: It is actually too simple for us. We wanted deeper configuration options in the
first run — segments, alerts, advanced filters. We felt constrained by the guided flow.

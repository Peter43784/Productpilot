Sources: G2, product docs, pricing pages, news.

- **ACME Analytics** — onboarding copilot launched Jan 2026: guided setup wizard, trial
  activation emails, per-seat pricing. Strong in mid-market. No sourced RICE, no audit trail.
- **BETA BI** — added onboarding playbooks and in-app checklists. Weak integrations (no Slack
  alerts until Q2). Legacy export still CSV-only.
- **GAMMA Insights** — AI setup wizard announced, GA expected Q2. Aggressive pricing for
  small teams. No competitive research agent, no persistent org memory.
- **Delta Reports** — focused on dashboards; onboarding is manual email templates. Cheap.
- Market: the "activation" tooling space is crowded in 2026; differentiation is depth of
  analysis + traceability, not feature checklists.

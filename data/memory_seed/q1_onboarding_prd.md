# PRD — Onboarding Redesign (Q1 2026) — approved

**Status:** shipped March 2026 · **Critic score:** 8.1/10 · **Owner:** Priya (PM)

## Problem
New trial signups stalled at the workspace-setup step; trial activation was 12%.

## Segment
New trial signups, 40-200 seat B2B SaaS teams (approx. 1,200 trials/quarter).

## Success metric
Trial activation rate (trial -> active workspace) from 12% to 25% within two quarters.

## Sourced RICE (from 24 tickets + 14 reviews + 18 NPS)
- Onboarding wizard simplification — RICE 14.2, High confidence
- Activation email delivery fix — RICE 11.8, High confidence
- Guided first-run tour — RICE 8.9, Med confidence

## Decision
Shipped wizard simplification (5 fields max) + activation email fix. Guided tour deferred.

## Lessons
Teams cite the first run as decisive. Keep onboarding to three steps. Revisit guided
tour in Q3. This is the canonical onboarding reference for future decisions.
